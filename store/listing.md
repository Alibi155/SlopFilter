```
SlopFilter for LinkedIn
```

## Short description (132 char max)

```
Greys out AI slop and humblebrag posts in your LinkedIn feed. Learns what you consider slop. Runs entirely on your device.
```

## Detailed description

```
Your LinkedIn feed is full of posts that were not written so much as generated: emoji-bulleted
listicles, "It's not X. It's Y." one-liners, fake-bold headlines, and "Agree?" at the bottom of
every one. Mixed in with them are the humblebrags — "humbled and honored to announce" — that say
nothing you needed to know.

SlopFilter greys those posts out.

Not hides. Not deletes. Greys out — the post stays exactly where it is, dimmed, with a small chip
telling you why it was flagged and quoting the text that triggered it. Hover to read it normally.
You are always in control of what you see.

WHY IT WAS FLAGGED, ALWAYS
Every flag comes with its reasons, in plain language, quoting the actual phrases. No mystery
scores.

IT LEARNS YOUR TASTE
Disagree? Click "This was no slop" and it un-dims. Spot something the rules missed? Mark it. Those
corrections train a small model that runs in your browser, and it gets better at your particular
definition of slop the more you use it. You can also add your own keywords.

RUNS 100% ON YOUR DEVICE
SlopFilter requests no network permission at all. It cannot send your feed anywhere, because it
cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. Your
corrections and your model never leave your browser.

OPEN SOURCE
Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license, including the full list of what it looks for. Read
it, change it, or open an issue if it gets something wrong.
```
